About Viking

Marine service for the systems vessels rely on.

Viking Engineering supports ship owners, vessel operators and shipyards with hands-on service for critical onboard systems.

Work includes servicing, troubleshooting, testing and calibration across turbine and pump systems, instrumentation and controls, safety equipment, cargo and tank systems, and electrical and mechanical equipment.

Project support can cover work-scope planning, schedules and manpower, tools and spares, yard permits and risk assessments, followed by service or calibration reports.

What we do

Marine service capabilities

Browse the systems we work on and the service tasks available for each group.

01

Turbines & pumps

Service and operational support for turbine-driven machinery.

  • Turbine generator safety devices and operation
  • Cargo pump turbine safety devices and operation
  • Feedwater pump turbine safety devices
02

Instrumentation & control

Testing, servicing and calibration for electrical and pneumatic systems.

  • Control valves, controllers, positioners and actuators
  • Pressure, level and temperature transmitters and sensors
  • Float switches, level gauges and level controllers
  • Engine-room instruments and calibration equipment
03

Safety & monitoring

Service and operational support for vessel detection and alarm systems.

  • Inert gas systems: service, calibration, testing and operation
  • Fire, smoke and pump-room gas detection systems
  • Portable gas meters, bilge alarms and high-level alarms
  • Temperature monitoring and vibration systems
04

Cargo, tank & environmental

Support for vessel tank, ballast and discharge monitoring equipment.

  • Ballast and tank pressure and level systems
  • Pneumatic level gauges and controllers
  • Salinometer system service, testing and calibration
  • ODME servicing and operational support
05

Electrical, mechanical & HVAC

Field service, testing and installation for supporting equipment.

  • Electrical motor servicing and testing
  • Electrical panel wiring and termination
  • HVAC control panel troubleshooting and service
  • Incinerator servicing and testing; stainless steel and copper-tube installation
06

LNG & project support

Technical work and practical coordination for shipyard projects.

  • LNG compressor safety devices and PLC upgrades
  • Coordinate work scope, schedules, manpower, tools and spares
  • Lead daily technician and fitter assignments
  • Prepare yard permits, risk assessments and service reports
